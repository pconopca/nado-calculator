S2 Weekly Pool
How the Season 2 pool works

Every week (one epoch), Nado releases a points pool split among all traders by their share of points. Unlike Season 1's fixed emissions, this pool grows with trading volume.

The higher the platform's average daily volume that week, the bigger the pool — following the curve below.

Your cut = your points ÷ everyone's points. This calculator multiplies the weekly pool by your number of farming weeks to project your total.
