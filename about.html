Methodology & data sources

Every number on the calculator comes from somewhere — this page says exactly where. Where the source is Nado's own docs or API, we say so. Where a figure is a public estimate or an assumption you control, we say that too.

1What this is

nadocalc.xyz is an independent, community-built tool that estimates the $INK airdrop value for Nado trading points. It's not affiliated with Nado or Ink — all trademarks belong to their respective owners. Nothing here is financial advice.

2Points supply — what's confirmed vs. estimated

DistributedOfficial
24,941,776 points across Private Alpha, Off-Season, and Season 1 — the exact figure published in Nado's docs.
ReservedEstimated
2,000,000 points (Builder Codes 1M + Comp/Reserve 1M) — from Nado's public bonus-pool announcement. Trading Comps (originally 4M) was removed and isn't counted.
Season 2 weekly poolLive
In Live mode: the average of the last 4 completed S2 epochs, read directly from Nado's public API (archive.prod.nado.xyz). In Custom mode: a theoretical curve built from the volume/pool data points Nado published, letting you model a volume you choose yourself.
Epoch close timeVerified
Every Friday at 01:00 UTC — cross-checked against the end_time field the API actually returns for each epoch, not assumed.

3How your $ value is computed

Your estimated value is a simple three-step chain — no hidden multipliers:

your share = your points / total points pool airdrop pot = FDV × airdrop % // both are your inputs your value = your share × airdrop pot

Total points pool = Distributed + Reserved + (S2 weekly pool × weeks farmed). The weekly pool depends on which mode you're in (see above).

4What's not confirmed

Airdrop %Unconfirmed
No official number exists yet. We show a projected 3–10% of $INK supply based on public discussion — every dollar figure downstream inherits this uncertainty, which is why we surface it as a range, not a single number.
$INK FDVYour assumption
Fully diluted valuation is whatever you type in — $INK hasn't launched, so there's no market price yet. Change it freely to model different scenarios.
Total $INK supplyUnannounced
Not public. That's exactly why the calculator works off FDV instead of a token count × price.

5On-chain wallet tracking

When you paste a wallet address (or a .eth / .ink name), the calculator queries Nado's own public points API directly from your browser — the same data anyone could query themselves. Names are resolved to addresses first via Blockscout's public BENS service (chain 1 for .eth, chain 57073 for .ink).

Nothing is sent to us. There's no backend and no server — this is a static site. The only place your address is ever written is your own browser's local storage, and only to power two conveniences: remembering your wallet so it auto-loads next visit, and comparing your point count to your last visit so we can show what changed. Clear it any time with the "Forget this wallet" button.
